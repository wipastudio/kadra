How the game works

A photo surfaces from someone's library. Everyone else guesses whose it is. The app runs a programmed night — a warm-up, a main block, a change of pace and a finale — rather than a loose stack of rounds.

The shield turn

Before a photo goes to the room, it appears on its owner's screen alone for 1.5 seconds. Let it through, or veto it and nobody else ever sees it. The room learns only that a shield was used, never what was on the photo. Vetoing costs points, so it stays a real decision rather than a reflex. You can turn the shield off in Settings if your table prefers to play fast.

Which photos are in play

You choose: your whole library, specific albums, or photos you pick one by one. Regardless of what you choose, the app never offers:

  • screenshots and document scans,
  • anything taken in the last 48 hours,
  • photos in your Hidden album — iOS never shows those to the app at all,
  • photos you have previously marked "never show this one again".

Every candidate is also checked with Apple's on-device Sensitive Content Analysis before it can be shown.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Kadra has no accounts, no sign-in and no password. You pick a nickname the first time you open it, and that is all.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos are never stored on any server. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what happens in each mode.

How many people can play?

Two to eight, on separate iPhones or iPads. There is also a solo practice round that teaches the game on one device — it is clearly labelled as practice, and the answers in it are made up because all the photos are yours.

Do we need internet?

Not if you are in the same room. Kadra finds nearby phones over the local network (the same Wi-Fi is enough) and nothing leaves your device. If you are apart, you play by sharing a link, and then an internet connection is required.

Does everyone need to buy Premium?

No. One person at the table is enough — everyone else plays for free in that room.

Someone behaved badly. What can I do?

Any player can report a photo or block another player from the results screen. Blocked players cannot be admitted to your rooms again. Reports and blocks are stored on your own device; the block list survives until you clear it.

How do I delete everything Kadra stored?

Settings → Your data → Everything → Delete. That clears saved nights, the never-show list, blocked players and reports. Nothing is left behind, because nothing was ever anywhere else.

The app says photo access is off

Open iOS Settings → Kadra → Photos and allow access. The game cannot work without photos. You can grant access to selected photos only; Kadra will use just those.

Prices aren't loading on the Premium screen

That means the App Store did not answer. Check your connection and try again — nothing is charged when this happens. If it keeps failing, email us.
