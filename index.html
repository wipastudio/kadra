How the game works

A photo surfaces from someone's library. Everyone else guesses whose it is. The app runs a programmed night — a warm-up, a main block, a change of pace and a finale — rather than a loose stack of rounds.

The Shield

If one of your photos comes up that you would rather not show, raise the Shield while it is being revealed: the photo leaves every screen at once and is never offered again. You get three per night and each one costs points, so it stays a real decision rather than a reflex. The room sees that a Shield was used.

Which photos are in play

You choose: your whole library, specific albums, or photos you pick one by one. From your whole library or an album, the app never offers screenshots, document scans, or anything taken in the last 48 hours; photos you pick one by one are used as you picked them. In every case it never offers:

  • photos in your Hidden album — iOS never shows those to the app at all,
  • photos you have previously marked "never show this one again".

When iOS's Sensitive Content Warning is on for your device, every photo is also checked with Apple's on-device Sensitive Content Analysis before it can be shown.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Kadra has no accounts, no sign-in and no password. You pick a nickname the first time you open it, and that is all.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

Game photos go only to the phones at your table: directly in the same room, end-to-end encrypted through a relay when you play apart, and they are not kept on any server. Two exceptions, each shown in the app before it happens: a photo someone reports reaches us for review and is deleted after 30 days, and sample frames reach us only if you switch them on. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How many people can play?

Two to eight, on separate iPhones or iPads. There is also a solo practice round that teaches the game on one device — it is clearly labelled as practice, and the answers in it are made up because all the photos are yours.

Do we need internet?

Not if you are in the same room. Kadra finds nearby phones over the local network (the same Wi-Fi is enough) and nothing leaves your device. If you are apart, you play by sharing a link, and then an internet connection is required.

Does everyone need to buy Premium?

No. Only the person who opens the room needs it — everyone else at that table plays for free.

Someone behaved badly. What can I do?

Report them, from the flag on the photo, from the player list (the people button at the top of the game screen) or from the results screen. Reporting also blocks them; you can block without reporting from the player list or the results screen. A blocked player leaves your table at once and cannot join your rooms again. Reports and blocks reach us, and we act on reports within 24 hours: the content is removed and the player who brought it is removed from rooms. You can also email us.

How do I delete everything Kadra stored?

Settings → Your data → Everything → Delete. That clears saved nights, the never-show list, blocked players, reports and your player ID, and deletes any sample frames you sent. Feedback and reports you sent are on our server for up to 180 days; email us with your install ID (Settings → Your data) to have them deleted sooner.

The app says photo access is off

Open iOS Settings → Kadra → Photos and allow access. The game is played with your photos, so only the sample round works without them. You can grant access to selected photos only; Kadra will use just those.

Prices aren't loading on the Premium screen

That means the App Store did not answer. Check your connection and try again — nothing is charged when this happens. If it keeps failing, email us.
