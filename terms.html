4. Playing with other people

Kadra is a game about showing your own photos to people you invited. You are responsible for what you put into a game.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You accept these rules before you can play, on a screen shown the first time the app opens.

  • Only share what you are comfortable sharing. Each round opens with the photo shown to you alone for 1.5 seconds so you can veto it. If you turn that shield off, photos in your chosen scope are shown without asking.
  • Do not share photos of other people without their consent, and do not share content that is unlawful, sexually explicit, hateful, violent, harassing, or that depicts a minor in any sexual or exploitative way.
  • Do not harass, threaten or bully other players.

How we keep objectionable content out

  • Filtering. Candidate photos are screened on your own device with Apple's sensitive content analysis before they can enter a round. A photo that is flagged is dropped from the game and replaced, and in room play it is not sent to the other players at all. No image leaves your device to be analysed. Where the system's sensitive content policy is turned off, the veto and the controls below are what protect the table.
  • Flagging. Any player can report a photo or a player from two places: the player list during a game (the people button at the top of the game screen) and the results screen at the end of the night.
  • Blocking. Any player can block another player from those same two places. Blocking takes effect at once: that player's photo leaves the table immediately, their part in the round is voided, and they cannot join your rooms again. If you are the host, blocking also removes them from the room.
  • Reports reach us. A report is sent to Wipa Studios with the reason you picked and anything you wrote. No photo is sent, and no player name is sent. The report screen lists exactly what is sent before you send it.
  • We act within 24 hours. We respond to reports of objectionable content within 24 hours of receiving them: we remove the content and eject the user who provided it.

Photos are relayed between players and never stored by us, so we cannot look at a photo after a game ends — which is why the report you send, and the steps above, are what we act on. If someone behaves in a way that needs our attention, you can also email wipastudios@gmail.com with what happened. We will respond, and we may refuse service to accounts or devices involved in abuse.
